About me

I am a Humboldt Research Fellow at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany, hosted by Prof. Heinz Konietzky. I received my PhD from Central South University under the supervision of Prof. Ming Tao.

During my doctoral studies, I was a joint PhD researcher at the University of Melbourne with Prof. Guillermo A. Narsilio. I also worked as a guest researcher at Monash University, collaborating with Prof. P. G. Ranjith.

More about me

I have published more than 50 papers in journals including International Journal of Rock Mechanics and Mining Sciences, Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering, Engineering Geology, Journal of Rock Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering and Computers and Geotechnics. My publications include five ESI Highly Cited Papers (top 1%) and two papers in Nature Index-listed journals. I have also contributed to more than 30 Chinese invention patent applications.

I serve as Associate Editor for Frontiers in Earth Science and Guest Editor for Applied Sciences. From November 2026, I also serve as Guest Editor for Deep Resources Engineering. My reviewing service includes International Journal of Rock Mechanics and Mining Sciences, Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering, Journal of Rock Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering, Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology, International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, and Computers and Geotechnics.

I am listed among the World’s Top 2% Scientists in the 2026 Stanford/Elsevier ranking for single-year scientific impact.
